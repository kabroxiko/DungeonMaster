Build the campaign on this map:
- Anchor `campaignConcept`, `majorConflicts` and `dmHiddenAdventureObjective` in the regions named above, using their
  names as written.
- **Every settlement, stronghold or site you name must be one of the Named places above, spelled exactly as written.**
  The players can see those names on their map, so an invented place name contradicts it. Refer to a location you
  need by the nearest named place and a local detail ("the shrine a mile outside the nearest town").
- **Use each name as the kind it is.** A region is a territory: write "in the region of X", never "the city of X" or
  "the market of X". Only settlements have streets, gates, markets and docks. Lakes, rivers and mountains are landforms.
- Do not invent a region, coastline or mountain range. A settlement the campaign needs but this block lacks does not
  exist; keep anything new small and local, inside one of the named regions.
- Never rename, translate or respell a name from this block.
