Build the campaign on this map:
- Anchor `campaignConcept`, `majorConflicts` and `dmHiddenAdventureObjective` in the regions named above, using their
  names as written.
- **Every settlement, stronghold or site you name must be one of the Named places above, spelled exactly as written.**
  The players can see those names on their map, so an invented place name contradicts it. Refer to a location you
  need by the nearest named place and a local detail ("the shrine a mile outside the nearest town").
- **Use each name as the kind it is.** A region is a territory: write "in the region of X", never "the city of X" or
  "the market of X". Only settlements have streets, gates, markets and docks. Lakes, rivers and mountains are landforms.
- **Every place sits in one region.** Each named place states the region it belongs to ("— in X"). Keep that pairing when
  you place a conflict, a faction or a secret: a place belongs to the region the block names and to no other.
- **Surroundings and terrain are the ground itself.** Every region names the terrain most of its land is, and every
  place names the ground around it (terrain, bordering terrain, coast, river, lake, mountains, island). Let that decide
  what a region can grow, mine, fish or trade and where a faction would sit. Terrain forbids features, it does not just
  suggest them: a region or place reported as **desert** has no river, stream or woods, and an inland place has no
  harbour — describe wells, cisterns, dry wadis or caravan roads instead. Never contradict the block.
- Do not invent a region, river, coastline or mountain range. A settlement the campaign needs but this block lacks does
  not exist; keep anything new small and local, inside one of the named regions.
- Never rename, translate or respell a name from this block.
