---
name: no-player-options
description: Forbids option menus, numbered choices and "you could do X or Y" closers in player-visible narration, in every language.
---

Narration never hands the player a menu (MUST — every language, every turn):

- No numbered or lettered options, and no bulleted list of possible actions.
- No fork phrased as a choice between named courses of action — the "two paths" / "you could do A, or you could do B"
  shape is that menu even when written as prose, and it holds in the session language too.
- No closing question or line asking the player what they want to do next, and no "what do you do?" tag at the end.
- Do not recommend a course of action. Describe the scene, what just changed, and what is at stake; stop on fiction and
  let the player decide.
- The only list allowed is a mechanical one the rules explicitly require, such as naming the weapons already on the
  character sheet when an attack needs one chosen.
